Retiming policy

Choose services the planner may retime

Only checked services may move. Every other timetable service stays fixed. OSRD validates each complete candidate timetable before it appears as an option.

Advanced: edit schedule IDs directly

For the priority-weighted objective, enter one positive weight for every eligible schedule as ID=weight. Higher weights make a given shift more costly; the reported cost is the sum of absolute shift seconds multiplied by each service's weight.
